Notify Group — Message Format Options

A — Current (plain list)

SplitBot

💰 Outstanding Balances — Trip to Japan

@alice owes @bob JPY 3,000.00
@charlie owes @alice USD 18.50
@kevin owes @alice JPY 1,500.00

Simple, but reads like a raw data dump. No visual hierarchy.

B — Arrows + bold amounts

SplitBot

💰 Trip to Japan — outstanding balances

@alice → @bob  JPY 3,000
@charlie → @alice  USD 18.50
@kevin → @alice  JPY 1,500

Arrow makes the direction clear. Bold amounts stand out. Group name prominent at top.

C — Grouped by who owes

SplitBot

💰 Trip to Japan — outstanding balances

@alice owes:
  • @bob  JPY 3,000

@charlie owes:
  • @alice  USD 18.50

@kevin owes:
  • @alice  JPY 1,500

Easy to scan your own name. Gets wordy with many debts.

D — Compact with call-to-action

SplitBot

💰 Trip to Japan

@alice → @bob  JPY 3,000
@charlie → @alice  USD 18.50
@kevin → @alice  JPY 1,500

Tap below to settle up ↓

Adds a nudge to open the app. Works well if the bot message has an inline button attached (could be added later).

E — Grouped by currency

SplitBot

💰 Trip to Japan — outstanding balances

JPY
@alice → @bob  3,000
@kevin → @alice  1,500

USD
@charlie → @alice  18.50

Useful when the group has many currencies. Feels clean but less personal — names are less prominent.
