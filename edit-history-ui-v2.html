3 · Admin panel: deleted expenses and full history

Deleted expenses never show in the Telegram app, but the admin can see and restore them. The Expenses list gets a “Show deleted” switch; deleted rows are greyed and struck through with who deleted them and when; every row can expand to its full history. This matches the existing admin look.
