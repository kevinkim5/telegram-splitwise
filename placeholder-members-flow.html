Placeholder members — how it would work

Nothing is built yet. Walkthrough from “add a friend who isn't on Telegram yet” to “they claim their spot”.

A · Adding someone who hasn't joined

Today you can only split with people who have already messaged the group or opened the app. With placeholders, anyone in the group can add a name, optionally an @username, and start splitting with them straight away.

D · Claiming a spot after starting the app

Anyone Telegram confirms is in the group can claim, so strangers can't. First open: the app registers them as a normal member, then asks whether they are one of the people who were added for them. They see exactly what they're taking over before confirming.

E · Zero-tap version: matching by @username

If the person who added them entered a username, and that same user later opens the app (or sends any message in the group), they're linked automatically. No claim screen. They just see a banner with an undo, in case the match was wrong.

F · Lifecycle & the awkward cases

1 · Created by any group member (name, optional @username). Counts in splits, balances, settlements like anyone else. No Telegram account needed.
↓
2a · ClaimedThey pick their name in the app, or the @username matches.Everything the placeholder owns (paid-for expenses, shares, settlements, who-did-what in history) moves to their real member record, in one atomic step. The placeholder disappears.
2b · Never joinsNot everyone will.They stay as a ghost for the whole trip so money still adds up. Anyone can record a payment on their behalf (“Annie paid Kev SGD 40, in cash”). Once their balance is zero, a group member can remove them.
↓
Already registered before claimingThe common case.They open the app, get registered normally, then claim. So claiming is really a merge of two member records. If both appear in the same expense, their two shares are added together.
DuplicatesTwo people added “Annie” and “Annie L.”A “Same person as…” action merges placeholder into placeholder, before anyone claims.
↓
Wrong person claimsWithin a friend group, low risk but it can happen.The confirm screen shows exactly what they take over; the claim is logged in Activity with who/when; the person who added the placeholder is told. There is no automatic “unlink”, because by then expenses have merged. Fixing a wrong claim would be a manual admin job, so the confirm step does the real prevention.
